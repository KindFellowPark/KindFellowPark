# Andrew Park

Executive assistant. I spent seven years as executive assistant to a university president, and
I now support a college's marketing and public relations office as its only administrative
staff member. I build AI workflows for the office's recurring work by directing Claude, and I
keep a person's review in front of anything that reaches an executive.

Most of what I build runs on work and household data, so those repositories stay private.
What's public here are clean copies made to show how the work is done:

- **[meeting-minutes-workflow](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/meeting-minutes-workflow)**:
  turns a meeting transcript into formatted minutes in Word, with a name check that stops for
  my confirmation before anything is drafted. Minutes that typically took two to three hours by
  hand now take about ten minutes from a labelled Teams or Zoom transcript.
- **[curator](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/curator)**: a two-person household app for
  logging what we watch and choosing what's next, with sign-in limited to an approved list and
  a spreadsheet kept as the record.

Also in use, not public: a Claude skill that builds the monthly report to the Board of Trustees
in Canva, which a colleague now runs himself.
