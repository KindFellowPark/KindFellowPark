# Andrew Park

Executive assistant who automates the mechanical parts of the job. I spent nearly seven years
as executive assistant to a university president, and I now support a college's marketing and
public relations office as its only administrative staff member. I build AI workflows for the
office's recurring work by directing Claude, and I keep a person's review in front of anything
that reaches an executive.

Most of what I build runs on work and household data, so those repositories stay private.
What's public here are clean copies with made-up data, made to show how the work is done:

- **[meeting-minutes-workflow](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/meeting-minutes-workflow)**:
  turns a meeting transcript into formatted minutes in Word, with a name check that stops for
  my confirmation before anything is drafted. Minutes that typically took two to three hours by
  hand now take about ten minutes from a labelled Teams or Zoom transcript.
- **[jarvis-assistant](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/jarvis-assistant)**: an executive
  assistant for an executive assistant. My partner and I text it over iMessage, because a text
  never gets skipped the way email and notifications do. It handles the calendar, mail drafts,
  bills and trip plans, and sending an email, removing an event or adding a bill waits for a
  yes by text.
- **[board-report-skill](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/board-report-skill)**: a Claude
  skill that builds the monthly report to a Board of Trustees in Canva, with every story
  approved by a person before it is placed. A colleague now runs it himself.
- **[budget-dashboard](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/budget-dashboard)**: merges a
  spending tracker with the finance system's exports into a one-page budget view for my
  director and me, and flags spending on accounts nobody is tracking.
- **[accessibility-checks](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/accessibility-checks)**:
  accessibility rules the document tools follow while writing, and a check that tests the
  finished file and reports what it found in plain words.
- **[curator](https://github.com/KindFellowPark/curator)**: a two-person household app for
  logging what we watch and choosing what's next, with sign-in limited to an approved list and
  a spreadsheet kept as the record.
